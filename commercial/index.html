Kitchen Climate Control

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Fort Collins HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm proper venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Fort Collins

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling quickly, throughout Fort Collins and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on commercial equipment. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back fast. After that, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to prevent a repeat, and each response comes with full documentation for your facility files.
